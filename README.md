
# 🕵️ Digital Detective

**Digital Detective** is a cinematic detective investigation game where every clue matters. Investigate mysterious cases, examine evidence, question suspects, reconstruct timelines, uncover contradictions, and use logical reasoning to solve crimes.

🔗 **Play Now:** [https://detectivdigitl.netlify.app/](https://detectivdigitl.netlify.app/)

## 🎮 Available Cases

### ✅ Case 001: The Missing Necklace
Investigate the disappearance of a valuable necklace. Examine the evidence, explore the suspects' motives, and uncover the truth behind the theft.

### ✅ Case 002: The Locked Room Mystery
A scholar is found unconscious inside a study locked from the inside. Investigate the suspicious circumstances, analyze forensic evidence, and uncover the truth behind the seemingly impossible incident.

### ✅ Case 003: The Stolen Painting
A priceless painting disappears during a gallery blackout. Analyze the evidence, challenge suspect alibis, and expose the contradictions that reveal the culprit.

## 🧩 Gameplay Features

- 🔎 **Evidence Investigation:** Discover and inspect clues to uncover hidden details.
- 🕵️ **Suspect Analysis:** Examine suspect profiles, motives, and statements.
- 🕰️ **Timeline Reconstruction:** Piece together events to understand what really happened.
- 🔗 **Clue Connections:** Connect evidence and identify contradictions.
- 📓 **Detective Notes:** Organize your findings throughout the investigation.
- ⚖️ **Final Deduction:** Use the evidence to identify the culprit and explain the crime.
- 📂 **Multiple Cases:** Explore different mysteries, each with its own story and investigation.
