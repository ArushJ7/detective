CONFIDENTIAL WITNESS LOG // DEPOSITIONS

1. MARCUS FINCH (ASSISTANT) — DISCOVERY STATEMENT

“I brought the second draft of the cryptographic monographs at precisely 21:40. The bolt was thrown on the inside. I knocked three times. Then came a violent shudder, a cry, and the sound of porcelain shattering. Inspector, the key was turned tight inside the escutcheon—I could see its barrel through the keyway.”

2. MRS. AGNES GREY (HOUSEKEEPER) — FINAL CONTACT

“I served the Master his Earl Grey and dried dates on the silver tray at 20:55. He was deep in calculation over his cipher desk. As I stepped onto the gallery, he said, ‘Lock the outer vestibule, Agnes; tonight we prove the impossible.’ That was the last word he spoke to me.”

3. DR. J. ALISTAIR (COLLEAGUE) — PHYSICAL EVIDENCE REMARK

“Adrian was not a man given to hysteria or suicide. But his desk smelled distinctly of bitter prussic distillate and autumn aconite. Yet the cup sat unbroken on the center blotter with not a drop spilled outside the rim.”
