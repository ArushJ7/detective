EXHIBIT A: PRELIMINARY SYNOPSIS

On the evening of 14 October, Lord Archibald Blackwood hosted an intimate gathering for four esteemed associates to present the 120-carat Queen of Solms necklace. The gem was contained in an armored glass vitrine with two keyed locks: Key A held by Lord Blackwood, Key B by his sister, Lady Eleanor.

At 22:30, lightning grounded the generator transformer. The entire manor was plunged into darkness for exactly seventeen minutes. When candles were recovered at 22:47, the cabinet was unlocked, unmarred, and empty.

Neither key left its respective owner. How was the case opened without violence?
