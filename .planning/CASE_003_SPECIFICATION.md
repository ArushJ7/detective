## 2. CANONICAL TRUTH

### Canonical Solution
- **Canonical Culprit:** **Julian Vance** (38), Senior Curator & Master Art Restorer at Ravenwood Gallery.
- **Canonical Motive:** **£12,000 Gambling Debt & Illicit Art Syndicate Collusion.** Vance accumulated astronomical debts with the Blackwood Syndicate due to high-stakes illicit card games. To prevent his public disgrace and violent retaliation, Vance agreed to deliver the original canvas to an underground buyer in exchange for wiping his debt plus a £3,000 cash bonus.
- **Canonical Theft Method:**
  1. **Preparation (21:15 GMT):** Vance pre-loosened the rear gilt turn-screws securing the canvas within frame `EVD-301` and manually disengaged the trip-wire connecting the Main Hall emergency mechanical gong (`EVD-303`).
  2. **Blackout Trigger (21:17–21:18 GMT):** Vance slipped down to the Sub-Basement Electrical Room and used graphite-lubricated pliers to trip the primary gas-engine dynamo fuse breaker (`EVD-302`), plunging the entire gallery into total darkness at 21:18 GMT and disabling the electrical frame wire contact loop.
  3. **Canvas Dismount (21:20–21:24 GMT):** Wearing soft felt-soled slippers (`EVD-304`), Vance unlocked Alcove III with his curator's key, quickly dismounted the unfastened canvas, inserted a pre-cut sheet of dark velvet into the empty frame to conceal the theft in dim ambient light, and rolled the canvas inside a leather protective sleeve.
  4. **Concealment (21:24–21:27 GMT):** Vance carried the canvas to the Restoration Corridor and concealed it inside a hollow brass chemical storage cylinder (`EVD-306`) tucked into a recessed wall cavity.
  5. **Alibi Lock-In (21:28 GMT):** Vance entered the Restoration Room, locked the heavy soundproof oak door from the inside, and waited until Guard Arthur Pendelton manually reset the dynamo breakers at 21:30 GMT.

### Decisive Alibi Contradiction
- Vance swore under oath that he was trapped inside the locked Restoration Room during the entire 12-minute blackout (21:18–21:30 GMT) and specifically claimed:
  > *"I was locked inside the Restoration Room when the lights failed. I sat at my desk until I distinctly heard the emergency chime ring twice in the Main Hall at 21:24 GMT, assuring me the guards were responding."*
- **The Physical & Acoustic Impossibilities:**
  1. **Acoustic Soundproofing (`EVD-305`):** The Restoration Room door is a heavy, double-padded felt-sealed oak door. Acoustic testing proves that sound from the Main Hall is attenuated by over 45 dB when the door is locked—making any hall gong completely inaudible from inside the sealed room.
  2. **Disengaged Gong Cable (`EVD-303`):** Forensic examination of the emergency gong assembly in the Main Hall reveals that its mechanical pull-cable was physically unhooked and pinned back at 21:15 GMT—meaning **the gong never chimed at 21:24 GMT at all**. Vance fabricated hearing a specific chime that physically did not occur!

## 3. BLACKOUT MECHANISM & SECURITY SYSTEM

### The 12-Minute Blackout Window
- **Power Failure Start:** `21:18 GMT`
- **Power Restoration:** `21:30 GMT`
- **Total Duration:** `12 MINUTES` (720 seconds)

### Gallery Electrical & Security Architecture (1896 Technology)
1. **Gas-Engine Electric Dynamo:** Located in the Sub-Basement Electrical Room. Generates direct current (DC) for 40 carbon-arc ceiling lamps throughout the gallery halls.
2. **Low-Voltage Frame Alarm Circuit:** A secondary battery-powered circuit runs through copper contact pins on the mounting brackets of high-value paintings. If a frame is lifted off its bracket, the circuit opens, dropping an indicator flag on the annunciator board in the Guard Lodge.
3. **Blackout Vulnerability:** The low-voltage alarm circuit receives its primary trickle-charge directly from the dynamo switchboard. When the primary breaker is shorted at 21:18 GMT, **both the ceiling lights AND the frame alarm circuit fail simultaneously**.
4. **Gas Footlights:** Low-pressure gas footlights along main corridor baseboards remain dimly lit during electrical failure, providing minimal silhouette visibility (~0.5 lux) but no direct detail recognition.
