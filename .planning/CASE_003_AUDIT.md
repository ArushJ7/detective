## 2. BLACKOUT AUDIT
- **Status:** **PASS**
- **Analysis:**
  - Duration: Exactly 12 minutes (`21:18 GMT` to `21:30 GMT`).
  - Mechanics: The main DC knife switch on the gas-engine electric dynamo switchboard (`EVD-302`) feeds both the 40 ceiling arc lamps and the low-voltage frame contact alarm loop. Shorting the primary breaker cuts both lighting and alarms simultaneously.
  - Ambient Illumination: Low-pressure gas footlights along baseboards remain lit, providing minimal ambient glow (~0.5 lux). This explains how Vance could navigate with memorized gallery geometry while ensuring the black velvet backing inserted into frame `EVD-301` concealed the missing canvas in shadow.

## 4. ALIBI AUDIT
- **Status:** **PASS**
- **Analysis:**
  - Culprit Julian Vance claims he was locked inside the Restoration Room for the entire 12-minute blackout and distinctly heard the emergency hall gong chime twice at 21:24 GMT.
  - **Decisive Contradictions:**
    1. `EVD-305` (Acoustic Door Test): Demonstrates 48 dB acoustic attenuation through the padded oak door, making hall gongs inaudible inside the sealed room.
    2. `EVD-303` (Disengaged Gong Wire): Proves the gong pull-cable was unhooked at 21:15 GMT, meaning **the gong never chimed at 21:24 GMT at all**.
  - The contradiction is fair, logical, discoverable, and forces the player to question Vance's presence without immediately revealing the entire theft mechanism.
