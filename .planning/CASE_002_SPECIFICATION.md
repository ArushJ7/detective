## 2. CANONICAL TRUTH

### Canonical Solution
- **Canonical Culprit:** **Dr. Evelyn Reed** (Senior Research Associate & Former Protege)
- **Canonical Motive:** Prevention of Exposure & Academic Theft (£10,000 Patent & Fraud). Dr. Reed plagiarized Professor Cole's groundbreaking alkaloid synthesis patent and sold the rights to a London syndicate. Cole discovered the financial discrepancy at 18:00 and threatened to notify the Royal Society at midnight.
- **Canonical Method:** **Delayed Neurotoxic Contact Poisoning via Contaminated Brass Wax Seal Stamp.**
  - At 20:30, while briefly left alone in Cole's study under the pretext of returning a monograph, Dr. Reed coated the handle of Cole's personal brass wax seal stamp with a concentrated fictional neurotoxic compound mixed with a transdermal carrier reagent.
  - At 21:03, Professor Cole entered his study alone, habitually locked the door from the inside with the heavy brass deadbolt, and sat down to seal his letters to the Royal Society.
  - Handling the stamp at 21:10 caused transdermal absorption. Between 21:20 and 21:30 (estimated central collapse at ~21:25), Cole suffered acute neuromuscular paralysis, dropping his pen and collapsing near his desk.
- **How the Locked-Room Illusion Was Created:**
  - **The room was locked by the victim himself at 21:03**, long before he collapsed during the 21:20–21:30 window.
  - The culprit did **NOT** lock the door from the inside after an attack, nor did she use wires, strings, or secret doors.
  - To fabricate an alibi for the time of the collapse, Dr. Reed set up a **delayed mechanical decoy sound** at 20:30: she positioned a heavy bronze paperweight on a sloped desk ledge balanced by a specially calibrated tallow candle stub set to burn through a retaining gut string at approximately 21:42.
  - When the weight fell at 21:42 with a loud thud, witnesses (including suspect Julian Sterling outside) assumed an attack was happening *at that exact moment* (21:42), when Dr. Reed was visibly playing chess in the Drawing Room!

## 3. LOCKED-ROOM MECHANISM & ILLUSION

### Physical Detail
1. **The Door Lock:** Standard 1890s Victorian mortise deadbolt with a solid brass thumbturn handle on the interior side. When thrown from inside, the bolt extends 1.5 inches into the reinforced iron strike plate. It cannot be operated from outside without a master key, and even with a master key, if the key is left in the interior keyhole, an exterior key cannot engage the tumbler.
2. **The Windows:** Heavy double-hung sash windows equipped with interior brass lever catches ("acorn latches"). Both windows were securely latched from the inside.
3. **The Contaminated Stamp Transmission:**
   - Forensic neurotoxin residue + transdermal carrier reagent.
   - Applied to the turned ebony handle of the **Brass Wax Seal Stamp** (Item `EVD-204`).
   - Latency: 10–20 minutes after skin contact before respiratory failure and collapse occurs in the 21:20–21:30 window.
4. **The False Timestamp Decoy (The 21:42 Thud):**
   - At 20:30, Dr. Reed positioned a heavy bronze sphinx paperweight (`EVD-208`) on the edge of the high slanted reading lectern.
   - She bound it with a thin gut string tied to the wick base of a calibrated tallow candle stub (`EVD-209`).
   - At 21:42 (approximately 72 minutes after ignition at 20:30), the calibrated candle burned down to the string, severing it. The heavy weight plummeted 6 feet onto the brass fender of the hearth, creating a sharp, metallic "THUD" heard across the courtyard.
   - This tricked Julian Sterling and the household into believing Cole was struck or attacked at 21:42, establishing an airtight alibi for Dr. Reed, who was sitting in the Drawing Room in front of three witnesses between 21:15 and 21:50.

### Discovery Clues for the Mechanism
1. **`EVD-204` (Contaminated Wax Seal Stamp):** Forensic neurotoxin residue on the handle; Cole's thumbprint etched into the wax residue.
2. **`EVD-209` (Burned Candle Stub & Charred String):** Found on the high lectern desk with melted tallow and a tiny scorched fragment of violin gut-string, inconsistent with standard candle wicks.
3. **`EVD-208` (Sphinx Paperweight):** Found lying inside the brass hearth fender with a fresh metallic scratch matching the fender rim, 8 feet away from the desk where it normally sits.
4. **`EVD-207` (Autopsy Report / Medical Examiner Note):** Notes transdermal absorption on Cole's right thumb and forefinger with an estimated collapse window between 21:20 and 21:30 (~21:25 central point), **NOT 21:42**.
