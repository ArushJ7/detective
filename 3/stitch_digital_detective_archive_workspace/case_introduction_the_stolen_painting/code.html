MEMORANDUM FOR: Inspector Reginald Vance, C.I.D., Scotland Yard.
SUBMITTED BY: Desk Sergeant G. Finch, West End Division.
DATE: 14th November 1896, 22:15 Hours.

At precisely 21:18, heavy vibration from the mechanical dynamo room ceased abruptly. Gallery patrons and personnel were gathered in two separate salons when illumination expired. The gallery's primary gas regulator was manually shut at the central valve, requiring a brass spindle key retained exclusively by Head Guard Pendelton and Curator Vance.

Upon restorative lighting at 21:30, inspection of Alcove III revealed the frame of "The Astronomical Quadrant" empty. Picture hooks and wires remained hanging without deflection. A faint scent of chloroform or turpentine was detected near the lower skirting board. All exterior egress points were verified locked from within.

"Your objective is to identify who moved through the dark, who possessed the bypass tool, and where the excised canvas was concealed before the dawn raid."
